# Case: Standard software via a reseller, GGD Hollands Noorden

> Public tender, not anonymised. Source: TenderNed, publication TN-580905 (28 May 2026), [tenderned.nl/aankondigingen/overzicht/426720](https://www.tenderned.nl/aankondigingen/overzicht/426720). Year: 2026.
>
> Worked out from the public notice by GovTechNL as a starter case: a procurement of **licences, not of a service**. The challenge owners may add or replace cases.

## The system or service

GGD Hollands Noorden (a public-law body in the health sector, based in Alkmaar) wants to buy **standard software** through a **reseller**, by placing individual follow-on agreements (*nadere overeenkomsten*) under one framework agreement.

## The data

The notice is about acquiring software licences. Which data ends up in the software depends on which products are bought and how they are used. That is exactly the question for your tool.

## Criticality

Depends on the products. Office software and security tooling at a public health service can be critical for daily operations.

## Current situation

Not described in the notice.

## What was asked in the tender

- Open procedure under Directive 2014/24/EU, set up as a framework agreement with one participant and without renewed competition.
- Estimated value EUR 14 million excluding VAT, over six years (three years plus three optional one-year extensions).
- Award: quality 77 points, price 23 points.
- Submission deadline: 18 September 2026.

## Questions for teams

- The reseller supplies licences; it does not run the software. Which autonomy requirements can you set for a reseller at all, and which belong with the software maker or the hosting party?
- Does your tool handle a procurement where most requirements for a SEAL level do not apply? What does it show the buyer?
- How would the advice change for one specific product bought under this framework, for example a cloud-based office suite?
