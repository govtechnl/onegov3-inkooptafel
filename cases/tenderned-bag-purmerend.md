# Case: SaaS BAG application, gemeente Purmerend

> Public tender, not anonymised. Source: TenderNed, publication TN-569372 (30 January 2026), [tenderned.nl/aankondigingen/overzicht/413896](https://www.tenderned.nl/aankondigingen/overzicht/413896). Year: 2026.
>
> Worked out from the public notice by GovTechNL as a starter case: a **small, single-purpose** procurement. The challenge owners may add or replace cases.

## The system or service

A multi-tenant **SaaS application for the BAG** (*Basisregistratie Adressen en Gebouwen*, the national register of addresses and buildings) for four municipalities: Purmerend, Oostzaan, Waterland and Wormerland. Gemeente Purmerend procures on behalf of all four.

The scope also covers:

- integrations with existing applications and national registers and facilities (LV-BAG, BGT, a GIS viewer and DDS);
- implementation, testing, data conversion and end-user training;
- cloud hosting, support, maintenance and advice.

## The data

Mainly register data on addresses and buildings, most of which is public through the national BAG. Check for yourself whether personal data is involved (for example in case files or user accounts) and what that means for your classification.

## Criticality

The BAG is a statutory base register: municipalities must keep it up to date and deliver changes to the national register. Work out how critical a short or longer outage is for that task.

## Current situation

The notice describes a replacement by one supplier for four municipalities. Contract duration 48 months with two extension options. Estimated value EUR 200,000 excluding VAT.

## What was asked in the tender

- Open procedure under Directive 2014/24/EU.
- Award: quality 80 points, price 20 points.
- Submission deadline: 13 March 2026, 12:00.

## Questions for teams

- This is a small procurement for one module with mostly public data. Which SEAL level fits, and how many requirements from the full set for that level does a buyer really need here?
- Which requirements remain relevant because the service is shared by four municipalities and connects to national registers?
- How does your tool show the buyer why it leaves requirements out?
