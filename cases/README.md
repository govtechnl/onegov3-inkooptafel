# Procurement cases

Procurement cases to demonstrate your tool on (criterion S1). Each case follows [`_template.md`](_template.md).

| Case | Scope | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Forensic medicine application, GGD](ggd-forensische-geneeskunde.md) | Replace one in-house application with highly sensitive data | A Dutch GGD, anonymised, via the challenge owners | Available |
| [SaaS BAG application, Purmerend](tenderned-bag-purmerend.md) | One SaaS module for four municipalities, mostly public data | TenderNed TN-569372 | Available |
| [Standard software via a reseller, GGD Hollands Noorden](tenderned-standaardprogrammatuur-ggd-hn.md) | Licences through a reseller, not a service | TenderNed TN-580905 | Available |
| [HenT, gemeente Amsterdam](tenderned-hent-amsterdam.md) | Hosting and technical management of 126 applications | TenderNed TN-534511 | Available |

The cases differ on purpose. The core of the challenge is a requirement set that fits the scope of the procurement: a small procurement on a high SEAL level should not get every requirement for that level. You may also pick your own public tender from [TenderNed](https://www.tenderned.nl); describe it with [`_template.md`](_template.md).

## Rules

- The GGD case is anonymised. Do not try to identify the organisation, people or suppliers involved. The TenderNed cases are public notices.
- Your worked example goes in `submissions/<team-name>/`, not in this folder.
